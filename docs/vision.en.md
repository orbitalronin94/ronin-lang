# Part VIII — RONIN’s future

This is an English companion summary of the Spanish source in [`vision.md`](vision.md). The master specification marks sections 8.1–8.5 as “no structural changes”; their missing prior-edition text is not reconstructed here.

The section frames RONIN as a systems language, orchestration layer, ecosystem, and possible substrate for neuromorphic and autonomous systems. Its closing koans emphasize self-designing systems, conversational architecture, and the transition from a static equation to a family of models.
